# Northwind Coffee brand profile (fictional example)

Last updated: 2026-09-04 by SoCal. Sources: fictional example for format reference only.

## 1. Business snapshot
- What they sell and to whom: specialty coffee subscriptions and three cafes, to urban 25 to 45 year olds who care about origin and craft.
- Business model and price point: premium; subscriptions from $24 a month, cafe average ticket $9.
- Markets and languages: Pacific Northwest, English.
- Business goals social must support: grow subscriptions 30% by Q2 next year; lift cafe weekday footfall 10% this quarter.
- Key competitors: Blue Bottle, local roasters, Trade Coffee subscriptions.
- Decision makers: founder (final say), marketing manager (day to day).

## 2. Brand foundations
- Purpose: make the origin of every cup visible.
- Positioning: for curious coffee drinkers, the roaster that shows you the farm behind the bag, unlike big specialty chains, because we buy direct and tell the whole story.
- Personality: curious (asks questions, explains without lecturing), warm (people first, never snobby), precise (real numbers, real names), understated (confidence without hype).

## 3. Audience
- Segment 1, the curious upgrader: 25 to 35, drinks good coffee, wants to learn without being talked down to; Instagram and TikTok; responds to process videos and taste explainers; turned off by gatekeeping.
- Segment 2, the ritual regular: 30 to 45, cafe visitor three or more times a week; Instagram and email; responds to people and place content; turned off by constant promotions.
- Segment 3, the gift buyer: seasonal; Instagram and Pinterest; responds to clear bundles and unboxing.

## 4. Voice and tone
- Voice in one sentence: a well-travelled friend who roasts coffee and would rather show you than tell you.
- We sound like: specific, warm, plain. We never sound like: a sommelier, a discount retailer, a motivational poster.
- Words we use: origin, farm names, roast dates, "taste for yourself", "this week's harvest".
- Banned words: artisanal, curated, elevate, indulge, "coffee lovers", "obsessed".
- Formatting: sentence case, one emoji maximum per caption and never in the first line, US spelling, three hashtags at most.
- Tone shifts: complaints are answered fast, plainly and with a fix; crises use no humour; education uses questions.
- Example captions: "This bag left Huila 19 days ago. Here is what happened in between." / "We tried a 12-day rest on the Ethiopia. Sweeter. Tell us if you agree." / "Weekday mornings at Pike: quieter, same beans."
- Miss: "Elevate your morning ritual with our curated selection" (banned words, no specifics, wrong register).

## 5. Visual identity
- Logo: wordmark only on feed; clear space equal to the N height; never on busy photos.
- Primary colours: Roast Brown #3B2A20, Cream #F4EFE6.
- Secondary: Leaf Green #4F7A45, Ember #C9672B.
- Typography: headline Fraunces, body Inter; files in the brand Drive folder.
- Photography: natural light, hands and process, real cafes, warm grade, no stock.
- Video: unhurried, 3 to 6 second shots, captions in Cream on Roast Brown block, first frame is always a close-up of the bean or the pour, no trending audio unless instrumental.
- Accessibility: alt text describes origin and process; contrast checked against Cream.

## 6. Channel footprint
- Instagram @northwindcoffee: 12.4k, primary channel, 3 posts a week achieved, Reels of process win, static promos lose.
- TikTok @northwindcoffee: 2.1k, launched this year, two posts a week, tasting explainers work.
- Pinterest: dormant, gift season opportunity.

## 7. Guardrails
- Off-limits: politics, health claims about caffeine, jokes about other roasters.
- Claims needing sign-off: any "direct trade" or price-paid-to-farmer figure, sourced from the founder.
- Regulated: none beyond standard advertising rules; food safety statements need the operations lead.
- Competitor mentions: none by name.
- Disclosure: creator content labelled as paid partnership; gifted bags disclosed.
- UGC: written permission via DM before reposting; credit in caption.
- Approval: marketing manager within 24 hours; founder for anything with farmer names or numbers.

## 8. Content pillars and proof points
- Origin and craft (40%): farm stories, roast decisions, tasting explainers; Reels and carousels.
- People and place (30%): baristas, regulars, the cafes; Reels and Stories.
- Offers and drops (30%): new harvests, subscription bundles, seasonal gifts; Reels, Stories with links, Pinterest.
- Proof points: buys direct from 11 farms across 4 countries; roast date on every bag; subscription churn under 4% a month (founder, internal).
